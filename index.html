About Me

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at the University of Virginia, advised by Prof. Yen-Ling Kuo in the Link Lab. My research focuses on robust robot learning, tactile and force-regulated manipulation, and human–AI/robot collaboration. I am particularly interested in enhancing AI models' perception and understanding of the physical world, with the goal of building generalizable and robust robotic systems that can perform real-world tasks and collaborate effectively with humans.

Before UVA, I received my B.E. in Software Engineering (Embedded Systems) from the University of Electronic Science and Technology of China (UESTC), and worked on machine learning and embedded AI systems in industry.

Research Interests

  • Robust robot learning: adapting pre-trained robot policies and vision-language-action models so they stay reliable under distribution shift
  • Tactile and force-regulated manipulation: low-cost tactile and force-controlled hardware, and policies that regulate contact force for delicate objects
  • Human–AI/robot collaboration: physically-grounded collaboration with diverse human partners
